Welcome to 3D Printing Education and Exploration

Why 3D Printing

Join with me in the exciting adventure of 3D printing. This is a compelling technology that takes ideas and models from the computer world and brings it into reality.

Whether you are familiar with 3D printing, starting on your printing journey, or just looking at possibly getting into printing we are here to expand your current understanding in a fun and enjoyable way.

Aspects of the word Hobby

Depending on your personality and your objectives can effect the way you look at 3D printing.

If you are mechanically minded and like to tinker with things then tinkering with 3D printers are the hobby for you.

If you are more of a creative type then designing and printing your own creations is the hobby for you.

Just always remember to keep in mind why you are looking at 3D printing for a hobby. Printers can break down and need fixing, which you can do! If you not interested in fixing/tinkering you can always spend more for a more friendlier model that does not require tinkering.

Kinds of 3D Printers

There are 2 popular kinds of printers available:

  • SLA Printers (Resin)
  • FDM Printers (Filament)
